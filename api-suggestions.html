Fewer classes that overlap

The largest gains. A reader who cannot tell two classes apart cannot choose between them, whatever they are called.

Six text layout classes 9.0.0, one merge

Finding

TextLine, CompositeTextLine, TextBox, TextBlock, TextFrame and TextColumn, plus Paragraph. TextBox and TextBlock share thirty setters and differ in eleven. TextColumn is a second paragraph flow beside TextFrame, with its own spacing names.

Suggestion

Merge TextBlock into TextBox now, a union of setters. Fold TextColumn into TextFrame once its CJK paragraphs and line between paragraphs move over. Two classes fewer.

Touches Example_01, 02, 10, 23, 27, 29, 31, 44, 49 and Cell.setTextBox, setTextBlock, setTextColumn.

Table and BigTable are two table APIs 10.0

Finding

One says setData and drawOn(pdf, pages, pageSize). The other says setTableData, setNumberOfColumns, setTextAlignment, complete and getPages. Both draw a table over many pages.

Suggestion

Give them the same names, or make streaming a mode of Table. At the least, setData, setTextAlignmentInColumn and drawOn should mean the same in both.

Touches Example_43 and the seven Table examples.

Chart data is a list of lists of Point 10.0

Finding

Point carries a marker, a path role, series text, a link and even a table cell alignment. A series has no object of its own, so its colour, width and label live on its first point.

Suggestion

Give Chart an addSeries like BarChart, with the stroke, marker and label on the series. Point keeps x, y and the marker. The largest change here; it can wait for the next major version.

Touches Chart, Point, Cell.setMarker, Example_09.

Hide what is not for users

Everything here is visible in the generated references and in code completion, and none of it helps someone making a PDF.

Java internals 9.0.0

  • PDF.append, newObj, endObj and getObjNumber; Encryption.getKey and getObjNumber; the AES128 and AES256 classes.
  • Barcode.drawOnPageAtLocation, called only by Cell.
  • ColorMap, used only by SVG parsing; the Swift copy exists only for reflection.
  • TextUtils.printDuration, used only by the examples, and the two font generator classes with main methods.
  • Util.readLines duplicates Content.ofTextFile, which the examples already prefer.

What to do in 9.0.0

Two items are left for this release, the TextBlock into TextBox merge and the Java internals; the two table APIs and the chart series wait for 10.0.

Leave TextColumn, the two tables and the chart series for 10.0. Each is a redesign, not a rename.
